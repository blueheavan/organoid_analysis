# Segmentation validation protocol (proposed; not executed)

**Current status: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE.** This document defines how
segmentation accuracy must be validated. It reports no results, because no
qualified independent annotation set exists in or alongside this repository.

## 1. Current evidence inventory (audited 2026-09-11)

| Asset | What it can support | Why it is not segmentation validation |
|---|---|---|
| 5 local TIFFs (`docs/validation_data_manifest.csv`) | Reader, runtime and execution smoke tests | No annotations; acquisition provenance unknown; stain and target identity undocumented |
| `data/synthetic_demo` truth masks | Pipeline arithmetic and matching code | Generated by the project with the same signal assumptions as the segmenter. Not independent and not representative of real optics |
| `validation/segmentation_metrics.py` | Hungarian IoU matching; P/R/F1, Dice, IoU, PQ, overlap-based split/merge indicators | Correct metric code is not evidence that segmentation is accurate |
| Cellpose MPS smoke run (2026-09-10) | Model executes on one crop | Accuracy was not evaluated |
| Web "review this segmentation" guidance | Visual inspection | Not blinded, quantified or reproducible |

## 2. Reference standard and qualification (required before any result)

- **Annotation:** manual 3D instance masks for each target (organoid envelope,
  cell, nucleus) drawn on the raw volumes at native resolution under a written
  SOP. The SOP defines boundary rules, the treatment of touching and
  border-truncated objects, lumen and hole policy (raw versus envelope), and
  minimum object size.
- **Independence:**
  - Annotators do not see model output. Annotation is **blind** to model masks
    and to treatment condition.
  - No annotated image may come from Cellpose model training data. Check the
    cpsam/cpdino training-set lists and record the result.
  - Parameter tuning (diameter, flow/cellprob thresholds, downsampling) uses a
    **calibration split** that is disjoint from the test split **at the
    biological-sample level**. The test split is evaluated once. Any reuse of
    it for design decisions ends its independence.
- **Inter-rater variability:** at least two independent annotators on a
  predeclared subset, for example ≥20% of fields covering every stratum. Report
  inter-rater F1, Dice and volume agreement. Model acceptance criteria may not
  demand agreement better than raters achieve with each other. Disagreements are
  adjudicated by a third annotator under the SOP.
- **Strata:** multiple independent biological samples (donors or lines),
  preparations, and at least the imaging conditions of the intended use:
  objective and voxel size, Z step and anisotropy, signal level, density and
  touching, hollow or lumen organoids, and border truncation. A result applies
  only to the strata that were sampled.

## 3. Evaluation units and aggregation

- The matching unit is the object. The **independent unit for uncertainty is
  the biological sample**, not the object or field. Confidence intervals use a
  cluster bootstrap over samples. Objects within a sample are never treated as
  independent replicates.
- Matching follows SCIENTIFIC_SPEC §9: one-to-one Hungarian assignment at IoU ≥
  0.5. IoU 0.3 and 0.7 are reported as a sensitivity analysis. The primary
  threshold is not changed after results are seen.

## 4. Metrics

**Object-detection level:**
- Precision, recall and F1.
- Missed objects (FN) and false-positive objects.
- Split errors: one reference object overlapped by ≥2 predictions, each
  covering ≥10% of it.
- Merge errors: one prediction covering ≥10% of ≥2 reference objects.
- Reported per stratum.

**Mask level:** Dice and IoU for matched objects. Boundary distance (mean
symmetric surface distance and 95th-percentile Hausdorff distance, in µm, using
physical spacing). Boundary distance is not implemented yet and must be added
and verified on analytical shapes before use.

**Downstream measurement level (primary for morphology claims):** for matched
pairs, the signed relative bias and limits of agreement of:
- volume (on the log scale);
- equivalent diameter;
- surface area;
- sphericity;
- principal-axis lengths.

Use Bland–Altman analysis with a CI for the bias and for each limit. Compute
surface area with the **same estimator on both masks**. That isolates the
segmentation effect from the estimator's own analytical bias, which is FAIL
separately (SG-1). Unmatched objects are reported as missing measurements and
are not dropped silently.

Dice alone never supports a claim that morphology measurements are validated.

## 5. Acceptance framework (numbers set by the project owner before data are seen)

For each claimed readout, predeclare:
- the smallest biologically relevant effect δ;
- the acceptance rule, for example an upper 95% CI bound on |bias| below δ/2
  and limits of agreement within ±δ;
- detection criteria on the lower CI bound of recall and of precision;
- the minimum evaluable N at the sample level, derived from the required CI
  half-width with a design effect for clustering.

**Illustrative only, not a criterion:** estimating recall of about 0.9 to ±0.05
needs about 140 objects without clustering. With intra-sample correlation, the
required count rises by the design effect 1 + (m − 1)·ICC.

If N at the independent level cannot reach the predeclared precision, the
outcome is INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, not PASS on a point estimate. No numeric
criterion is invented here, because the effect sizes depend on the study
question.

## 6. Reporting

Report the annotation SOP version, the annotators (by role), the blinding
record, the training-overlap check, the split manifest with hashes, the model
and version and all parameters, and per-stratum tables with CIs. Also report
failure examples, and a statement of which strata remain untested.
